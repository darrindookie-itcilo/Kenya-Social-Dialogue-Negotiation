# Kenya Social Dialogue & Negotiation Workshop

Participant dashboard · Naivasha, Kenya · 12–16 October 2026.

The interface follows the supplied ITCILO 2026 visual identity, with blue and cyan, Noto Sans SemiCondensed and ExtraCondensed typography, and the official logo on a white field at the right. The original workshop QR code is available on Welcome and the opening presentation’s dashboard screen (screen 9 in the default ten-screen sequence). Noto Sans is self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.

Open `index.html` in a browser, keeping the complete folder together. The participant site has no build step or external JavaScript dependencies. It is published on GitHub Pages. Live races connect to the existing secure Journey Mapper service; practice and the bargaining notebook remain local to the device.

The dashboard contains the five-day programme, ten opening screens (plus three optional confirmed-launch prompts), a participant activity desk, a standards library, Agreement Lab and a personal bargaining notebook with an illustrative cost calculator. Friday closes at 12:30. Times are East Africa Time (UTC+3).

Agreement Lab is a virtual card game; printing is optional. Open Agreement Lab, enter a team name if useful, and choose **Start Team Challenge** or **Open Projector Table**. Every round deals three move cards. Click or tap exactly two: they appear in the two spaces on the table. Click a selected card again, or its return button, to change your choice before revealing feedback. Write one reason; in Projector Table, a spoken reason can be confirmed instead. Select **Reveal & discuss**, then click a card to flip between its move and feedback. Continue through six rounds and export the team reflection from the results screen. Supported selected moves earn one learning point each, up to two per round; all three moves receive feedback.

Teams of 3–5 share one device per team. For a live race, open **Facilitator view**, sign in with the same ChatGPT account used for Journey Mapper, select a game and create a session. Share its participant link or eight-character code. Each team joins with an alias; its clock starts at joining. Agreement Lab counts a pair right when both selected moves are supported, and also displays learning points out of 12. The secure board shows current question, progress, correct/wrong counts and continuously running elapsed time. **Restart team** clears only that team's attempt and starts its clock again from zero. Team devices receive the restart automatically when connected. Participant devices cannot restart a live race themselves.

**Workshop games** is a separate sidebar tab containing the supplied V5 games: **Call It What It Is** (12 dialogue cards; maximum 240 points; Day 1, Session 3) and **The Dilemma Table** (five strategic issues and an options phase; Day 3, Session 1). They share the secure facilitator board. Call It What It Is reports forms right/wrong and settings right separately. The Dilemma Table reports value, trust and remaining mandate; its strategic choices are not marked right or wrong. Both rank by points, then total elapsed time. Leave the session code blank for practice. A team can use **Leave race** to return to practice while its race record stays on the board.

The board uses server-side authorization and allows only the workshop facilitator account. It contains no published password or password hash. Team connections use independent random tokens; each can read and update only its own progress. Submitted answers cannot be changed, and scores and start/finish times are calculated on the server. Delayed updates from an earlier attempt cannot overwrite a restart. Interrupted connections queue progress locally and retry. Live races require connectivity; offline results become visible when synchronization succeeds.

Projector Table remains available for whole-room discussion. The optional discussion timer can start, pause, resume, add 30 seconds or reset to 90 seconds, and never locks play. It is separate from the live race's total elapsed clock. Keyboard shortcuts: 1/2/3 select or flip cards, F toggles fullscreen, right arrow continues after discussion, Escape exits. Shortcuts do not intercept typing. If fullscreen is unavailable, use the browser’s fullscreen control (F11 on Windows). Practice reset clears the saved practice game after confirmation; exit preserves it. Previously saved practice choices remain usable.

Role tabs organize fictional exercise briefs. They are not access controls: downloads in this public repository remain public. Participants should open only the brief assigned by the facilitator. No real institutional secrets should be entered into the dashboard. Notebook notes and personal session checks save on the current device. A live race sends team aliases, game answers, progress and timings to the secure service; the notebook is not sent. Export notes before clearing or changing devices. “Clear notes” clears local notebook, practice game and personal checks; it does not delete facilitator race records.

Service-worker caching supports the dashboard after a successful online visit. PDFs become available offline only after they have been opened or downloaded; external official links require connectivity. The complete portable ZIP includes the local resources and works without a web server.

Participant activities were adapted from the supplied workshop materials. All simulation institution labels, mandates, disputes and numbers are fictional. Full instrument PDFs include final provisions; selected treaty/recommendation pages are extracted from official ILO publications or supplied official reference appendices. Kenya statuses were checked against NORMLEX on 30 September 2026. C155 and C187 full texts are linked online. The 1966 and 1997 education instruments are Recommendations, not ratifiable treaties.

The original PowerPoints, trainer notes, scripts, facilitator master packs, ceremony run sheets, certificate templates, unpublished handbook, emails, background sources and private editorial review are excluded from this repository. The optional handbook launch screens contain general event prompts, not the unpublished handbook text. ITCILO logo is used for the requested workshop branding.

Review actual current law, worker coverage, authority, SRC advice, lawful appropriation, approvals, registration and implementation arrangements for any real agreement. The cost calculator illustrates incremental costs; it does not establish affordability or legal authority. Game points are learning prompts, not a competency assessment.
